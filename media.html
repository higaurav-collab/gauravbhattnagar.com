Gaurav Bhatnagar

Gaurav Bhatnagar is an independent go-to-market consultant with 24 years across enterprise sales, founding a company and marketing leadership. He advises founders, boards and investors on whether marketing and growth stories hold up against revenue, including AI-driven claims. He writes The Silent Noise, a monthly newsletter on decision-making gaps in B2B marketing. He is a certified Independent Director (IICA).
