Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This page explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on gauravbhattnagar.com. It should be read with the Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can store in your browser. They are used to keep a site working, remember choices, count visits, or show advertising. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, do the same kinds of jobs.

2. What this site uses today

At the time of writing, this website does not set any cookies of its own. It does not use analytics, advertising, retargeting or social-media tracking tools. That means there is nothing for you to accept or reject, which is why you will not see a cookie banner.

CategoryUsed on this site today?What it would do
Strictly necessaryNone setKeep the site working, for example a security or session setting.
PreferencesNone setRemember choices such as language or layout.
AnalyticsNot usedCount visits and show which pages are read.
Advertising and marketingNot usedShow ads or follow you across other sites.

3. Third-party content and links

Pages load fonts and code libraries from Google Fonts, Bootstrap, jQuery and Cloudflare cdnjs. These requests normally do not set cookies in your browser, but the providers receive technical data such as your IP address, and their own policies govern what they do with it. Form submissions are handled by Formspree. If you follow a link to LinkedIn, or open the Google Calendar booking page, those sites may set their own cookies under their own policies.

4. If this changes

I expect to add website analytics once the site has been live and indexed for a while, so that I can see which pages help visitors. Before any analytics or other non-essential cookie is switched on, I will:

  • update this page with each cookie's name, provider, purpose and lifetime;
  • show a banner that asks for your consent, with accepting and rejecting made equally easy;
  • set nothing until you agree, and let you change or withdraw your choice at any time.

Search engines can index this site without any cookies, so there is no need to switch anything on for that.

5. Controlling cookies in your browser

Every major browser, including Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, lets you see, block and delete cookies in its settings. Blocking cookies can affect how other websites work.

6. Questions

If you have a question about this policy, write to higaurav@gauravbhattnagar.com.
