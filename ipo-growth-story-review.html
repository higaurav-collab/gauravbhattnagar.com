IPO Growth-Story Review

Pressure-testing the growth story before your bankers or SEBI do.

The DRHP is the one document a regulator, the press, and every analyst on the call will read line by line.

By the time a company is heading toward an IPO, the growth story stops being an internal pitch and becomes a public document: the DRHP, the roadshow deck, the analyst calls, all telling the same story to people with every incentive to pick it apart.

Jay Ritter's long-run data on IPOs, tracking more than 9,000 listings since 1980, shows newly public companies underperforming peers of the same size by an average of 3.6% a year for five years after listing. A lot of that gap traces back to a growth story that didn't survive contact with being public.

This review is a pressure-test of that story before it goes out: the same scrutiny SEBI, bankers, and analysts will eventually apply, run early enough to actually fix what doesn't hold up.

How This Works

Here's What This Actually Looks Like

  • A read of the growth narrative in the DRHP and roadshow deck against the numbers actually behind it, line by line.
  • Flagging claims that sound strong in a pitch but won't survive an analyst's first follow-up question.
  • Checking that growth drivers cited for the raise are ones the business can keep reporting on after listing, not one-time items dressed up as a trend.
  • A defined review window tied to the IPO timeline.

This is the same discipline I bring to the GTM Due Diligence for Investors work, applied from the other side: instead of reading someone else's growth story for a fund, I'm reading yours before the market gets to.

This is a pre-listing read, not investment banking, legal, or audit work, those stay with your own bankers, lawyers, and auditors. My job is the commercial and growth-narrative layer underneath what they're preparing.

Good fit if:

you're heading toward a listing and want the growth story in your DRHP or roadshow pressure-tested before analysts and the market do it for you.

Not a fit if:

you need the actual IPO process managed: investment banking, legal, or audit work. That's not this.

Frequently Asked

FAQs: IPO Growth-Story Review

What exactly do you review?
The growth narrative specifically: whether the drivers cited for the raise are real, sustainable, and consistent with what the company can keep reporting after listing.
How is this different from what my investment bank already does?
Banks build and sell the story. This is an independent read checking whether that story survives scrutiny, run alongside them, not instead of them.
What stage should I bring you in?
Before the DRHP is finalised, while there's still room to adjust the narrative, not after it's filed.
Do you handle any part of the actual listing process?
No. Banking, legal, and audit stay with your existing team. This is the commercial and growth-story layer underneath it.
What do I actually receive?
A written assessment flagging which growth claims are strong, which are exposed, and specific fixes for the latter.
Will this slow down our IPO timeline?
No, it's scoped to run alongside your existing prep, in a window that fits your filing schedule.
