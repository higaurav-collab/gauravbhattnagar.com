PE/VC Commercial Due-Diligence Advisory

Get a marketing-specific read on a deal, alongside your own team.

Is the CAC number in your target's deck real, or dressed up for the raise?

This service is built for PE and VC funds evaluating funding proposals who rarely have anyone in-house to dissect the marketing and scaling claims behind a deal. My review is built to answer exactly that kind of question before you commit.

I've done this work for an investment firm, evaluating funding proposals ranging from seed to growth-stage rounds. The investor and the specific deals stay confidential, as they should. What I was brought in for wasn't financial modeling; it was reading the commercial and go-to-market story a founder pitches and testing whether it holds up. Is the CAC number real or dressed up. Does the channel strategy actually scale the way the deck claims. Is the growth curve a marketing story or a market one.

Most diligence teams are built for financial and legal rigor. Very few have someone who's actually run the budget, hired the team, and been accountable for the number a marketing claim like this is supposed to produce. That's the gap I sit in.

How This Works

Here's What This Actually Looks Like

  • A defined review window tied to your deal timeline, not an open-ended engagement.
  • A written commercial assessment your investment committee can act on directly.
  • Direct conversations with the founding team where useful, testing the story behind the numbers.
  • Works alongside your existing legal and financial diligence process, not replacing it.

Good fit if:

you want a second, operator-level opinion on the GTM and marketing claims inside a deal before you commit.

Not a fit if:

you need financial or legal due diligence. That's outside what I do here.

Frequently Asked

FAQs: PE/VC Commercial Due-Diligence Advisory

What exactly do you review in a diligence engagement?
The commercial and go-to-market story behind the deal: whether the CAC and LTV numbers hold up, whether the channel strategy can actually scale the way the deck claims, and whether the growth curve is a marketing story or a real market one.
How is this different from the financial and legal diligence my team already does?
It runs alongside it, not instead of it. Financial and legal diligence test the numbers and the paperwork. This tests whether the commercial and marketing claims behind those numbers are real.
What stage of deals do you review?
Proposals ranging from seed to growth-stage rounds, the range I've evaluated for a past client, kept confidential as all my deal work is.
How long does a diligence review take?
It's tied to your deal timeline. Reviews are scoped as a defined window that fits your process, not an open-ended engagement.
Will you talk directly to the founding team?
Where it's useful, yes: testing the story behind the numbers directly with the people who built it, alongside document review.
What do I actually receive at the end?
A written commercial assessment your investment committee can act on, flagging specifically where the go-to-market claims hold up and where they don't.
How do you engage commercially?
Usually assignment-based, though I'm open to ongoing engagements. That call is entirely the investment firm's to make.
