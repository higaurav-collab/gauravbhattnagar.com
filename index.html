Executive Summary

24 Years, Both Sides of the Table

I have spent twenty-four years on both sides of the table: selling into enterprises, building marketing functions, and now advising leadership teams on go-to-market.

I started in enterprise IT sales, selling hardware, software and services and carrying accounts for global brands including ANZ Bank, Nestlé, and Barclays. In 2010, I left to found Chimmera Animations. It went on to beat a larger agency for a ₹75 crore national campaign mandate, and it made a holographic film that the Indian Army, and later the President and Prime Minister, personally recognised.

Since then, I have built marketing functions from scratch inside established organisations, most recently hand-picking and launching a 24-person consulting team in three months. I now advise founders and leadership teams on turning marketing spend into a number their board can actually trust.

I hold an Independent Director certification from the IICA. Today I split my time between fractional CMO work, GTM consulting, and board-level advisory, whichever a business actually needs, for as long as it needs it.
