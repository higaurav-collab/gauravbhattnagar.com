Executive Summary

24 Years, Both Sides of the Table

I have spent the last twenty-four years on both sides of the marketing function: running it as a corporate operator, building it as a founder, and now advising leadership teams that need one or the other.

I started in enterprise sales and business development, carrying accounts for global brands including ANZ Bank, Nestlé, and Barclays. In 2010, I left to build a creative business instead of inheriting one. It went on to beat a larger agency for a ₹75 crore national campaign mandate, and it built a holographic film that the Indian Army, and later the President and Prime Minister, personally recognised.

Since then, I have rebuilt marketing functions from scratch inside established organisations, most recently hand-picking and launching a 24-person consulting team in three months. I have also advised founders on turning marketing spend into a number their board can actually trust.

I hold an Independent Director certification from the IICA. Today I split my time between fractional CMO work, GTM consulting, and board-level advisory, whichever a business actually needs, for as long as it needs it.
